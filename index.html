THE STORY
Profit is 62% below budget because 70% of income rides on interest rates. Five actions add about AED 15.9m to 2027 profit.Rates fell, deposits left, transformation costs arrived early. The client franchise is growing but under-earning. The fix is on the fee and balance-sheet side.
